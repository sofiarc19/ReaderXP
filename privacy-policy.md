# ReaderXP Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

ReaderXP is a browser extension designed to enhance reading engagement through accessibility, personalization, and gamification features. We are committed to protecting user privacy and being transparent about how the extension operates.

## Information We Access

ReaderXP may access the following information when the user activates the extension:

- The title and URL of webpages used during reading sessions.
- Readable webpage content required to generate ReaderXP's reading view.
- User-configured settings such as font preferences, themes, accessibility options, and reading preferences.
- Reading session statistics and virtual pet progression data generated through use of the extension.

## How Information Is Used

The information accessed by ReaderXP is used solely to provide the extension's features, including:

- Generating a distraction-free reading experience.
- Tracking reading sessions and reading progress.
- Supporting accessibility and customization features.
- Maintaining virtual pet progress and user achievements.
- Saving user preferences between browser sessions.

## Data Storage

ReaderXP stores user settings and reading-related information locally using Chrome's storage APIs. Data remains on the user's device and is used only to support the functionality of the extension.

## Data Sharing

ReaderXP does not sell, trade, rent, or share user information with third parties.

ReaderXP does not use user data for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

## Remote Transmission

ReaderXP does not transmit webpage content, reading history, personal information, or user activity to external servers.

## Third-Party Services

ReaderXP does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## User Control

Users may remove stored ReaderXP data at any time by:
- Clearing extension storage.
- Removing the extension from their browser.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ReaderXP evolves. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
